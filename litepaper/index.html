02 // THE PROBLEM

Why Texas Needs Batteries

The Texas grid is under pressure from two directions at once: demand is growing fast, and supply is getting more volatile.

1. AI Is Hungry for Power

Data centers and AI training facilities require massive, reliable electricity. ERCOT projects that Texas power demand could grow 70% by 2031—most of that from new data centers. The grid wasn't built for this.

2. Renewables Need Storage

Texas has tons of solar and wind, but they're intermittent. Solar floods the grid at midday (sometimes pushing prices negative), then disappears at sunset right when everyone turns on their AC. This creates the "duck curve" problem.

The solution: Batteries charge when power is cheap and plentiful, then discharge when it's scarce and expensive. They smooth out the curve.

3. Regulations Are Tightening

Texas Senate Bill 6 now requires large data centers to disconnect during grid emergencies. AI companies can't just pause training mid-run—that destroys expensive compute jobs. They need grid stability, and batteries provide it.

04 // HOW IT WORKS

The Validator Model

This isn't a typical token sale. Here's the actual structure:

What You're Buying

A Validator License—an NFT on Solana that grants you:

  • Access to the Validator Dashboard
  • The right to participate in network validation (reviewing project data, voting on proposals)
  • Eligibility to claim $RDW tokens for active participation

You are NOT buying equity, debt, revenue share, or any claim on company assets. The license is a software tool, not a financial instrument.

What You Have to Do

This is not passive income. Validators must actively participate:

  • Monthly: Review project updates, confirm data accuracy (~30 seconds)
  • Quarterly: Vote on network proposals (~15 seconds)
  • Quarterly: Attend or watch the Validator Webcast
  • Annually: Renew your license (free)

Miss a requirement? Your rewards pause until you catch up. No participation = no tokens.

The Tokens

$RDW tokens don't exist yet. During the license sale, you accumulate "points." At the Token Generation Event (TGE), those points convert to actual tokens.

Token utility includes:

  • Governance: Vote on protocol parameters (not company decisions)
  • Access: Pay for premium grid data feeds
  • Staking: Lock tokens to increase validator weight

Important: Tokens may have zero market value. Don't assume they'll be worth anything.

07 // TREASURY OPERATIONS

What Happens When Projects Sell

When a project reaches NTP and sells to an institutional buyer, here's the flow:

  • Buyer pays USD to the asset SPV (ReDew Anson LLC)
  • SPV covers expenses, distributes profit to the operating company
  • Operating company may contribute a portion to the Protocol Treasury
  • Treasury may conduct market operations (buybacks) at DAO discretion
NO GUARANTEED BUYBACKS Treasury market operations are discretionary and subject to DAO governance. There is no promise, guarantee, or obligation to conduct buybacks. Any contribution from the operating company to the Protocol is voluntary.

Token holders never receive direct payments, dividends, or profit distributions. Any benefit comes indirectly through potential market effects of Treasury operations—which are not guaranteed to occur or have any specific outcome.

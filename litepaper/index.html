01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bridging DeFi & Energy

ReDewable Energy connects decentralized liquidity to real-world infrastructure. We fund the pre-development of utility-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) in ERCOT (the Texas grid operator).

Instead of a traditional token sale, we utilize a Capacity License (Node) model. Participants purchase a digital license that funds the high-risk, high-reward "Pre-NTP" phase. In exchange, holders receive daily emissions of the Network Reward Token ($RDW).

THE MISSION:

Stabilize the Texas power grid against rapidly growing AI and data center load by accelerating battery storage development.
02 // MARKET THESIS

The Perfect Storm

The Texas grid is under strain from two converging forces: accelerated load growth and volatility from renewables without enough storage.

1. AI Power Demand

AI and hyperscale data centers require large, reliable power. ERCOT has published load growth scenarios showing unusually high demand expansion.

2. The "Duck Curve"

Texas has abundant solar at midday and tight supply in the evening. The Solution: Batteries charge when power is cheap and discharge when prices spike.

05 // VALUE ACCRUAL

Supply Shock Protocol

When the physical asset is sold (NTP Exit), net proceeds are used to sweep the floor of the $RDW token. These tokens are not burned; they are sent to a Non-Circulating Vault (Lock).

WHY LOCK INSTEAD OF BURN?

Locking removes supply from circulation (creating price pressure) while keeping the tokens on the balance sheet for future DAO use (liquidity/collateral).

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bridging DeFi & Energy Infrastructure

ReDewable Energy connects decentralized liquidity to real-world energy infrastructure. We fund the pre-development of utility-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) in ERCOT (the Texas grid operator).

Instead of a traditional token sale, we utilize a Capacity License (Node) model. Participants purchase a digital license that funds the high-risk, high-reward “Pre-NTP” phase of development. In exchange, license holders receive daily emissions of the Network Reward Token ($TICKER) over a defined schedule.

THE MISSION:

Stabilize the Texas power grid against rapidly growing AI and data center load by accelerating battery storage development.

04 // VALUE ACCRUAL

The Supply Shock Protocol

Unlike simple "buyback and burn" models which destroy assets, ReDewable utilizes a Buyback & Lock mechanism.

When the physical asset is sold (NTP Exit), net proceeds are used to sweep the floor of the $RDW token. These purchased tokens are not burned; they are sent to a Non-Circulating Vault.

WHY LOCK INSTEAD OF BURN?

Locking removes supply from circulation (creating price pressure) while keeping the tokens on the balance sheet. This allows the DAO to use these assets later for deep liquidity provision or lending collateral without diluting holders.

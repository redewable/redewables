01 // THE THESIS

WHY BATTERIES MATTER

The Grid Is Maxed Out

Fact 1: Texas power demand is projected to grow 70% by 2031—mostly from AI data centers.

Fact 2: Extreme weather is getting worse. Deep freezes. Heat waves. The grid wasn't built for this.

When demand spikes and supply can't keep up, the whole system risks collapse. Batteries absorb the shock.

THE PROBLEM: NOT ENOUGH STORAGE

Data Centers Need Stable Power

Texas Senate Bill 6 now requires data centers to disconnect during grid emergencies. AI training can't just pause mid-run—that destroys expensive compute jobs.

Battery storage provides the frequency response that keeps voltage stable and data centers online.

The Solution: Batteries deploy faster than new power lines.
Our Role: We do the hard pre-development work others skip.

03 // THE ASSET

REDEW ANSON — FLAGSHIP SITE

Strategic Location

Located in West Texas near massive solar farms in the "Stargate Corridor." This area has a specific problem: too much power at midday, not enough in the evening.

When solar over-generates, prices crash (sometimes negative). Batteries charge cheap. When the sun sets and demand spikes, batteries discharge at premium rates. That's the business model.

The Grid Is Maxed Out

Texas power demand will grow 70% by 2031—driven by AI data centers. Add extreme weather (deep freezes, heat waves), and the grid faces collapse. Batteries absorb the shock, storing cheap solar energy and releasing it when demand spikes.

The Problem: Not enough storage to meet growing demand.

Data Centers Need Stability

Texas Senate Bill 6 now requires data centers to disconnect during grid emergencies. AI training can't pause mid-run—that destroys expensive compute jobs. Battery storage provides the frequency response that keeps voltage stable and operations online.

The Solution: Batteries deploy faster than new power lines.
Our Role: We do the hard pre-development work others skip.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

When will I start receiving rewards?
Rewards activate at the Token Generation Event (TGE). If you buy a license now, you are positioned for Day 1 emissions. Once live, rewards accrue daily and can be claimed via the dashboard.
How many tokens will I receive?
Each license earns approximately 110 tokens per day for 36 months. This is calculated by dividing the 300M reward pool by 2,500 licenses over 3 years.
Do I need to run a server or keep my computer on?
No. This is a "Virtual Node." The physical batteries are managed by our professional engineering partners (ANS/Enerzinx). Your license works automatically in your wallet to accrue rewards. No hardware or coding is required.
Can I sell my license later?
Yes. Your Capacity License is a standard Solana NFT. While we encourage long-term holding to maximize rewards, you can trade your license on secondary marketplaces (like Tensor or Magic Eden) at any time.
What exactly am I buying?
You are purchasing a "Capacity License" (NFT). This digital license proves you supported the pre-development phase. Holding this license in your wallet automatically qualifies you for daily token rewards once the network launches.
Is this legally compliant?
Yes. We operate under a strict legal framework. The sale is structured as a software license purchase, not an investment contract. However, we still implement KYC (Know Your Customer) for transparency.
Where does the money go?
100% of funds go to the development budget: securing the land title, paying Enerzinx for grid studies, and filing permits with ERCOT. You can view the full budget breakdown on the Capital Allocation section.
I need help. Can I talk to a human?
Absolutely. Our Concierge Support team is available 24/7 on Telegram to walk you through the process screen-by-screen.
